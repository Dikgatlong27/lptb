## Company overview

LPTB Solutions is a South African software and digital services company helping businesses establish their online presence, connect with customers and organise their operations. We design and develop websites, web applications, mobile interfaces and business platforms, supported by branding, integrations and ongoing technical assistance.

Led by founder and director **Tebogo Patrick Dikgatlong**, LPTB combines direct client engagement with practical software delivery. Our work spans hospitality, local delivery, education, retail and small-business services. Projects range from a business website or brand identity to the connected customer, restaurant, rider and administration systems of the YaBatho Delivery Platform.

Our approach begins with the client's immediate needs and a clear scope. We build in manageable phases, involve technical collaborators where the project requires additional expertise, and support clients as their systems develop. This gives growing organisations a direct relationship with the people planning and delivering their technology.

## Services and capabilities

### Websites and web applications

We create responsive company websites, product showcases, customer portals and dashboards. Our work includes information structure, interface design, React development, enquiry journeys and deployment support.

### Mobile applications and connected platforms

We develop mobile interfaces and application workflows using React Native and related tools. Our platform experience includes distinct customer, restaurant, rider and administrator journeys, connected through backend services and APIs.

### E commerce and payment integration

We support product catalogues, ordering journeys, cart and checkout interfaces, and payment integration. Scope can range from a product website to a connected commerce system, with hosting and third-party subscriptions defined for each engagement.

### Business systems and integrations

We plan and develop booking, operational and management workflows, authentication, databases and API connections. Backend, full-stack and specialist requirements are delivered with suitable technical collaborators. Learning-platform integration and early-stage CRM or ERP-style tools are offered according to agreed scope.

### Branding and business presentation

We provide logo design, company profiles, letterheads, invoice templates, product presentation and promotional graphics. These services help clients maintain a consistent identity across their websites, documents and customer communications.

### Maintenance and digital support

Ongoing support includes website updates, hosting coordination, email administration, document assistance and technical troubleshooting. Retainer arrangements define the covered services, systems and third-party costs.

### Business administration support

Our historical work includes assistance with company registration, beneficial ownership and annual return administration, supplier registration and related business documents. These services complement our digital work for small businesses.

The service offering is grounded in the existing company profile, website service descriptions and the client records in the project register. Offered capabilities should not be read as a claim that every service has already been delivered at enterprise scale.

## Leadership and delivery approach

Tebogo Patrick Dikgatlong directs client relationships, project planning, product decisions and delivery. His development journey began in 2021, with practical experience in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React and React Native. His work includes responsive interfaces, Firebase integration, API-connected application flows and deployment support.

LPTB operates with direct founder involvement and brings in trusted developers for backend, database, API and mobile requirements when needed. Team capacity and specialist responsibilities are defined for each engagement.

Our delivery process follows five stages:

1. **Understand the business.** Clarify users, priorities, available content and operational constraints.
2. **Define the scope.** Agree on deliverables, budget, dependencies and a realistic first phase.
3. **Design and build.** Develop the interface and connect the required services, with client review.
4. **Test and hand over.** Check agreed user journeys and prepare deployment and handover information.
5. **Support and improve.** Maintain the system and assess additional features as the business grows.

Leadership and project experience are described in the [director's professional CV](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1z-aJBLFfZNdmKdtYX6HvH4HsQkrkvdyp/edit).

## Featured project experience

### YaBatho Delivery Platform

YaBatho is LPTB's most substantial documented platform engagement. The system connects customer ordering, restaurant operations, rider delivery and administrative oversight through mobile applications, web interfaces and backend services.

The July 2026 handover records describe delivery of a customer mobile application, customer web application, restaurant application, rider application, administration dashboard, backend/API and public website. They describe the initial development phase as complete and moving into operations, maintenance and market rollout, with external distribution approvals and dependencies tracked separately.

LPTB led and coordinated development and technical implementation in collaboration with The Chairman Group and its C-Digital division. The commercial documents record an agreed project value of **R250,000**. Development is described as taking approximately 12–13 months; a **2025–2026** period is therefore approximate.

This engagement demonstrates LPTB's experience coordinating several user groups and operational workflows within one product. The stated project value is the platform's recorded commercial consideration, not a valuation of LPTB or of the YaBatho venture.
